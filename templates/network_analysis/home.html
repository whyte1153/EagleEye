Get the data (Kali)

  • Nmap (only on a network you own or are authorized to test):
    nmap -sV -oX scan.xml 192.168.56.0/24
  • Packet capture:
    sudo tcpdump -i eth0 -w capture.pcap
  • Safety: files are parsed, not executed. XML with DTD/entity tricks is rejected. Size, host, port and packet counts are capped.
  • Preservation: imports keep only parsed results. Store the original in the Evidence Vault if you need chain of custody.
